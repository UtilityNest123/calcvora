Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Calcvora (accessible at https://utilitynest123.github.io/calcvora/), visitor privacy is fundamental to our architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled when you use our simulators.

1. Client-Side Calculation Privacy

All calculators and simulators on Calcvora execute 100% locally within your web browser. Any numbers, hardware configurations, appliance loads, or financial figures you enter are processed strictly in your device's memory and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Local Storage & Preferences

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your visual theme preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode). You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep our tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Hosting & Server Logs

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical connection logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability purposes in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at utilitynest123@gmail.com.
