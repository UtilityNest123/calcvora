About Calcvora & Quantitative Methodology

Calcvora is an independent suite of engineering, artificial intelligence, and financial simulators created and maintained by Md Jobair as part of the UtilityNest web utility ecosystem.

Our Engineering Standards

Most free web calculators hide their assumptions behind black-box approximations. Every tool on Calcvora is built around three core principles:

  • Transparent First-Principles Equations: Whether calculating Transformer KV cache memory footprints, battery Depth-of-Discharge (DoD) derating, or nested payment gateway FX spreads, we publish the exact equations beneath every simulator.
  • 100% Client-Side Execution: All mathematical modeling runs locally inside your browser using JavaScript. We do not store, log, or transmit your appliance loads, hardware specs, or income figures to any external server.
  • Actionable Decision Outputs: Every simulator provides visual sensitivity charts, CSV data exports, and print-ready summaries for real-world planning.
